# Load Testing Strategy

## All 8 Scenarios

| ID     | Scenario             | Target TPS  | Duration | Warm-up                    | Cool-down | Pass Criteria                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Fail Criteria                                                                                                           |
| ------ | -------------------- | ----------- | -------- | -------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LT-001 | Baseline Performance | 1,200       | 30 min   | 5 min                      | 5 min     | p50≤30ms, p99≤100ms, error rate = 0% (sanity check: new architecture must comfortably beat the CURRENT system's own numbers at the CURRENT load)                                                                                                                                                       | Any error, or latency exceeding target even at 10x-below-capacity load                                                  |
| LT-002 | Target Load          | 12,000      | 60 min   | 10 min                     | 10 min    | p50≤30ms, p99≤100ms, p99.9≤250ms, error rate <0.1%                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Any budget line in `performance-budget.md` breached sustained for >3min (matches A5.6's own alert-threshold convention) |
| LT-003 | Burst Capacity       | 18,000      | 15 min   | 5 min                      | 10 min    | p99≤100ms maintained through the full burst window; no circuit breaker false-trips; error rate <0.5%                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Cascading failure (>1 circuit breaker OPEN simultaneously outside CB-FRAUD/CB-CACHE's expected occasional trips)        |
| LT-004 | Soak Test            | 9,600 (80%) | 4 hours  | 10 min                     | 10 min    | Heap growth <5% over the full duration (memory-leak detection); no gradual latency drift (p99 at hour 4 within 10% of p99 at hour 1)                                                                                                                                                                   | Monotonic latency increase or unbounded memory growth — either indicates a leak the shorter scenarios wouldn't surface  |
| LT-005 | Ramp-Up              | 0→15,000    | 30 min   | none (ramp is the warm-up) | 5 min     | Identify the inflection point (TPS at which p99 first exceeds 100ms) — the point itself is the deliverable, not a pass/fail gate                                                                                                                                                                       | N/A — diagnostic scenario                                                                                               |
| LT-006 | Failover Under Load  | 12,000      | 30 min   | 10 min                     | 5 min     | Zero committed transaction loss; p99 <5s during the failover window itself; full recovery within 30s RTO — **identical success criteria to chaos experiment CE-001** (docs/09), deliberately, so the load test and the chaos test validate the same claim under two different testing methodologies    | Any committed transaction loss, or recovery exceeding 30s                                                               |
| LT-007 | Mixed Workload       | 12,000      | 60 min   | 10 min                     | 10 min    | 60% P2P / 20% balance / 10% merchant / 5% reversal / 5% other; aggregate p99≤100ms across the full mix; reversal legs specifically complete within the 30s FR-005 SLA                                                                                                                                  | Aggregate p99 breach, or any reversal exceeding 30s                                                                     |
| LT-008 | Hot Partition        | 5,000       | 10 min   | 2 min                      | 3 min     | OCC retry rate on the hot shard stays bounded (per `account_balance_update_retries` exhaustion-rate alert threshold, A5.6: <0.1%/min sustained); other 31 shards' latency unaffected — **directly validates the same claim as chaos experiment CE-006** (docs/09), again deliberately cross-consistent | Retry-rate exhaustion alert fires, or non-hot-shard latency degrades measurably                                         |

Note the deliberate pairing of LT-006↔CE-001 and LT-008↔CE-006: the same architectural claim (graceful failover, hot-partition mitigation) is validated by two independent testing approaches — a sustained load test and a targeted chaos experiment — rather than treating them as unrelated checklist items. If they ever disagreed, that disagreement would itself be a signal worth investigating, per Section 22's cross-document consistency principle.

## Metrics Collection Strategy

Every scenario collects the full A5.6 metric set (`txn_requests_total`, `txn_processing_duration_seconds`, `kafka_consumer_lag`, `db_connection_pool_active`, `redis_memory_utilization`, `account_balance_update_retries`, `circuit_breaker_state`, `saga_state_transitions_total`) via the Prometheus scrape endpoint on each service (docs/03, Observability Stack). Dashboards used per scenario:

- LT-001/002/003: System Health Overview + Transaction Deep-Dive
- LT-004: Capacity Planning (specifically watching memory/heap trends over the 4-hour window)
- LT-005: Transaction Deep-Dive (watching for the specific stage that first breaches budget as TPS climbs — pinpoints the actual bottleneck, not just "latency went up")
- LT-006: Database Performance + System Health Overview
- LT-007: Business Metrics + Transaction Deep-Dive
- LT-008: Database Performance (shard-level breakdown specifically)

Full dashboard definitions: see `docs/14-architecture-review-deck.md` (Day 14).
